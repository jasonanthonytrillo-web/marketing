3. Purpose and Scope of Use

Elevate POS is used by the café for internal operational control, including:

  • recording orders and sales data for management monitoring;
  • inventory and raw-ingredient tracking;
  • staff, order, expense, and operational management; and
  • internal sales analytics and reports.

The system is not intended to replace the taxpayer’s BIR-registered invoice or other document issued to customers.

4. Receipt / Invoice Function

  • The system does not issue a customer-facing BIR invoice or receipt.
  • The receipt-printing feature has been removed or disabled from the deployed version presented to the BIR.
  • No system-generated slip is given to customers as proof of sale.
  • The café issues its BIR-compliant invoice separately through its approved/manual process.
Operational undertaking: The taxpayer will not use Elevate POS to print, issue, or represent any system-generated document as a BIR invoice or receipt unless the system is first evaluated and registered as required by the BIR.

5. Requested BIR Confirmation

The taxpayer respectfully requests the concerned Revenue District Office to evaluate the system as configured and confirm in writing:

  1. whether the system requires registration or a Permit to Use as a Cash Register Machine / Point-of-Sale machine or other sales machine;
  2. whether the system is considered a computerized accounting system, computerized books of accounts, or a component requiring separate registration; and
  3. what additional records, reports, configuration controls, or documentary requirements must be maintained.
