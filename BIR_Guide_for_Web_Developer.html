BIR Guide for a Web Developer

Elevate POS
How to request classification and registration guidance
Purpose: Use this guide to ask the BIR how the café’s configured system should be treated. This is not a legal opinion and does not replace a written BIR determination.

1. Which BIR office should you visit?

If you are asking about…Go first to…
The café’s use of Elevate POSThe café’s assigned Revenue District Office (RDO), based on the café’s registered business address. Bring the café owner or an authorized representative.
Your own developer/supplier accreditationYour own business’s assigned RDO, based on your registered head-office address. Ask for the Registration Section / eAccReg guidance for a supplier, developer, or provider.
Where to start inside the RDOGo to the Registration Section or Taxpayer Assistance / Officer of the Day counter. Say that you need guidance on POS/sales-machine classification and possible CAS/CBA registration.
Best approach: Have the café taxpayer-user make the first inquiry about its actual system use. You may attend as the developer and explain the technical configuration.

2. What to say at the RDO

Use this explanation:

“We are presenting Elevate POS, a web-based internal sales and inventory system used by a café. In the deployed version, the receipt-printing feature has been removed/disabled. The system does not issue customer-facing BIR invoices or receipts. The café issues its BIR-compliant invoice separately. We request confirmation whether the system requires a Permit to Use as a POS/sales machine, or registration as a computerized accounting system/component.”

Ask the officer to identify the applicable registration category and documentary requirements in writing or on the relevant BIR checklist.

3. What to bring

Bring the café’s documents

  • BIR Certificate of Registration (COR)
  • TIN and branch code
  • Business permit, if requested
  • Sample BIR-compliant invoice issued separately by the café
  • Authorization letter or board/owner authorization naming the person attending

Bring your system documents

  • Printed copy of the BIR_POS_System_Presentation.pdf
  • System name, version, release date, and developer details
  • Screenshots showing that receipt printing is removed/disabled
  • Live demonstration or test account
  • Sample internal sales, inventory, and management reports
  • Simple system flow diagram: order entry → internal report → separate BIR invoice
  • Written statement that no POS-generated slip is given to customers

4. Questions to ask the BIR officer

  1. Does this configured system require a Permit to Use as a CRM/POS or other sales machine?
  2. Because receipt printing is disabled, is the system treated only as an internal sales/inventory tool?
  3. Does the system qualify as a computerized accounting system, computerized books of accounts, or a component that must be registered?
  4. Does storing sales data or generating sales reports trigger a separate registration requirement?
  5. What exact BIR form, checklist, office, and online system should be used?
  6. Does the café need to submit eSales reports or any other reports for this system?
  7. What controls must be maintained for audit purposes, such as audit logs, user access, backups, and data retention?
  8. Can the RDO provide written confirmation or annotate the inquiry/receive a formal letter?

5. What not to do

  • Do not give the POS printout to customers as an invoice or receipt.
  • Do not label an internal report “Official Receipt,” “Sales Invoice,” or “BIR Receipt.”
  • Do not tell the BIR that the system is exempt; ask the BIR to classify it.
  • Do not disable printing only temporarily for inspection if the café will later use it for customer documents.
  • Do not claim that the system is “BIR-accredited” unless BIR has issued the applicable accreditation or permit.

6. After the visit

Step 1: Record the RDO, date, officer/section, requirements given, and any reference number.
Step 2: If registration is required, follow the official checklist and keep the filed application and acknowledgment.
Step 3: If the officer says no POS Permit is required, request that conclusion in writing or submit a signed letter and retain the received copy.
Step 4: Keep the deployed software version, configuration screenshots, reports, invoices, and change history together in one compliance folder.

7. Important distinction

PartyPossible BIR concern
Café / taxpayer-userWhether its actual system use requires PTU, CAS/CBA/component registration, and whether invoices are properly issued.
Web developer / supplierWhether you are supplying a sales machine/software subject to supplier/developer accreditation, especially if selling or deploying it for multiple taxpayers.
Bring this sentence with you: “Please classify the system based on its actual deployed functions and advise us of the exact registration, accreditation, or reporting requirements.”

Prepared for discussion with the concerned BIR Revenue District Office. Confirm current requirements directly with BIR because procedures and forms may change.
